Exam-Prep Engine

Search past exam questions, see why each one matched, practise a topic and get marked against a rubric.

Keyword search runs locally. Hybrid and semantic modes load a local embedding model on first use. Cited answers use a configured Gemini key and send your query and the retrieved question text to it. Sources are past questions, not official solutions.

AI mode: practice questions and rubrics are written by a language model from cited past questions (checked in code for IDs and marks only). Grades are AI feedback, not validated against human graders and not an exam mark.
